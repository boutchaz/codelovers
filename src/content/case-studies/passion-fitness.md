## The challenge

Running a class and attending it are different jobs. Coaches need an overview; members need a simple way to find their next session.

## What we built

We built connected experiences for club staff and members, bringing scheduling, bookings, attendance and training resources into the same product.

## The experience

Staff can organize the week while members book and follow their training from their phones. Each experience focuses on the person using it.
