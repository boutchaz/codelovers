## The challenge

A club’s busy evening spans tables, players and café orders. Separate tools leave staff piecing the activity together at closing time.

## What we built

We connected table operations, player cards, scoring and point of sale so the product follows the flow of a club’s service.

## The experience

Staff have a shared place to follow table activity and sales. Owners can review the close without reconstructing the evening from separate records.
