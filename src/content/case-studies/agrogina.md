## The challenge

Scattered records make it hard to connect what is happening in the field with what the team should do next.

## What we built

We brought farm management, mapping and agronomic information into one product, with web and mobile experiences for work at the desk and in the field.

## The experience

A farmer can move from a parcel to its observations and the work around it. The value is the connection between information and action, rather than another isolated dashboard.
