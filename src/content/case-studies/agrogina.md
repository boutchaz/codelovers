AgroGina helps cooperatives and mid-size farms (roughly 50–600+ ha) watch vegetation from space, manage parcels and teams, and keep stock and accounting in one place — including when connectivity is weak.

## The job

Farms across Morocco still mix WhatsApp, paper, and late field visits to know if a parcel is stressed. Scouting is slow and uneven. Ops (parcels, crews, stock, sales) live in other systems or spreadsheets. The goal wasn't a flashy demo — it was a tool cooperatives actually open in season, including off weak networks.

## What we set out to achieve

- A shared picture of parcel health before problems are obvious on foot
- Farm ops (parcels, teams, stock, accounting) next to that picture, not in a separate product
- Something that works for rural teams who lose signal for days, then sync when they're back
- Honest multi-cooperative use: each org only sees its own farms and people

## What the product delivers

- **Earlier eyes on the field** — Satellite vegetation views so stress shows up sooner than waiting for the next scouting trip
- **Parcels as the unit of work** — Boundaries, crops, history, and interventions in one place
- **Crews and roles that match the farm** — Agronomists, field agents, admins without shared chaos
- **Stock and sales tied to reality** — Fertilizer, equipment, and crop sales per cooperative
- **Field work that survives bad connectivity** — Record offline, sync later
- **Guidance, not only maps** — Trends turned into practical next steps farmers asked for

## Who it's for

Moroccan farm ops and AgTech buyers who need production software for the season — not a slide deck.
