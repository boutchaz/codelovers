## The challenge

A booking request can get lost between a conversation, a calendar and the team handling the next service.

## What we built

We connected WhatsApp conversations with availability checks and a booking dashboard, keeping the customer request and the operational record together.

## The experience

The workflow gives the customer a path to a reservation and the business a place to follow bookings and handle the next action.
